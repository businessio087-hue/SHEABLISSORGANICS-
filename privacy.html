01 / What we collect

Only the details
that help.

When you place an order, we collect the name, email address, phone number, and structured delivery details you provide. We also receive order items, quantities, totals, order and reference numbers, and payment status needed to fulfil and support the purchase.

We do not receive or store your full payment-card details. Yoco hosts the payment step and processes payment information under its own policies.

02 / How we use it

Made for a smoother
handoff.

We use order information to create and confirm your purchase, send a customer confirmation, send an internal order notification to SHEABLISS ORGANICS, arrange delivery, respond to support questions, and maintain accurate business records.

We may use limited technical information and browser storage to keep the site secure, remember essential choices, and understand broad site performance.

03 / When it is shared

With care,
not casually.

We share the information needed to complete your order with payment, email, hosting, database, and delivery service providers that help us operate the store. Providers receive only what is needed for their role and may process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect the website and customers, or to investigate suspected fraud or misuse.

04 / Your choices

Your information
stays yours.

You can contact us to ask about the personal information we hold about you, request a correction, ask questions about how it is used, or request deletion where applicable. Some information may need to be retained to complete a transaction, meet legal obligations, or resolve a dispute.

Cookie and browser-storage choices are explained in our Cookies Policy.
