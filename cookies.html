Small files, used thoughtfully. This page explains how SHEABLISS ORGANICS uses cookies and similar browser storage when you visit our site or use the ritual bag.

01 / What we use

Only what helps
the ritual run.

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. SHEABLISS uses essential browser storage to remember practical choices, such as whether you accepted this notice, and to keep the shopping experience working as you move between product selection and the bag.

We may also use limited analytics cookies or similar measurement tools to understand broad, aggregated patterns such as which pages are visited and whether the site is functioning as intended. We do not use cookies to store card numbers, security codes, or payment credentials.

02 / Your choices

Keep the choice
in your hands.

You can accept the site’s non-essential cookies with the button shown on the storefront. Your choice is saved in your browser so the notice does not keep returning. You can clear that saved choice at any time through your browser’s site-data or cookie settings.

Blocking essential cookies or local storage may affect the cookie notice, the ritual bag, or parts of the checkout experience. You can also use your browser’s private browsing controls or device-level settings to limit storage.

03 / Third parties

When another service
holds the hand.

Our hosted payment flow is provided by Yoco. When you choose to pay, you are redirected to Yoco’s secure checkout, where Yoco’s own cookies and privacy practices may apply. SHEABLISS does not receive or store your full card details.

Our site may load fonts and other assets from service providers, and social links can take you to Instagram, WhatsApp, or email services. Those providers operate under their own policies and controls.

04 / Questions

For questions about this policy or how your information is used, contact SHEABLISS8@gmail.com. We may update this page when the site, services, or applicable requirements change. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
