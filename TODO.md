# SHEABLISS ORGANICS — Todo

## Editorial single-page storefront
- The website is for SHEABLISS ORGANICS, an East African-inspired body care / self-care brand that fuses traditional aesthetics with modern skincare retail.
- Use minimalist editorial typography with a vintage / retro-modern aesthetic; use negative space, restrained copy, and type-led compositions; avoid busy or trendy UI flourishes.
- Use burnt terracotta, saffron/mustard yellow, deep espresso brown, muted teal accent, and warm cream/sand background; avoid pure white.
- Use woven raffia fringe, straw/rattan basket weave, kente-style striped cloth, carved dark wood, and natural fiber mat motifs.
- Use warm golden-hour lighting, high-contrast portraiture, rich melanated skin tones rendered with warmth, and editorial/fashion-magazine framing.
- Hero includes a full-bleed sunset-over-water or fringe-dress portrait image with overlaid brand name and one-line heritage-rooted self-care tagline; typography is treated as a design element.
- Brand story includes a short narrative on East African beauty traditions and natural ingredients, paired with a hands-holding-product-bowl image.
- Use the supplied hand-and-ice video as a muted section visual.
- Featured products include a skincare/body-care grid with warm wood/basket textures as card backgrounds and product photography on a neutral sand backdrop.
- Ingredient spotlight calls out natural/native ingredients including shea, baobab, and marula with icon-style illustrations in the terracotta/teal palette.
- Lookbook/lifestyle gallery includes 3–4 images mixing portraiture and texture shots such as woven mats, raffia, and headwrap portraiture.
- Testimonials use simple warm-toned cards.
- Newsletter/footer uses a dark espresso-brown background with cream text and a woven-pattern divider graphic.
- Copy is warm, proud, grounded in heritage, confident rather than exoticizing, and sparse/editorial.
- Interactions include subtle fade/slide-up animations on scroll, smooth hero image parallax, and soft product-card hover states with slight scale and shadow lift.
- Keep the site responsive and usable on mobile.
